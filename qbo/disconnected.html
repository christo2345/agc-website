AGC System for QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks is disconnected

AGC System no longer has access to the QuickBooks Online company. No more data will be read, and the connection keys held by AGC System are deleted.

How to disconnect

In QuickBooks Online, open Apps, find AGC System under My apps, and choose Disconnect. Project records already saved in AGC's system are kept as part of AGC's own business records.

Reconnect

The AGC QuickBooks administrator can connect again at any time from the connect page.

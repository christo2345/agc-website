AGC System for QuickBooks Online

End-user licence agreement

This agreement covers AGC System, an internal software tool made by American Graphic Contractors, LLC ("AGC") for its own use.

1. Who may use it

AGC System may be used only by AGC and the staff AGC authorises, and only with AGC's own QuickBooks Online company. It is not licensed, sold or offered to any other person or business.

2. What it does

It reads invoice, customer, product and payment information from AGC's QuickBooks Online company, and creates invoices there after an AGC project manager has approved them. It does not send invoices to customers and does not move money.

3. Acceptable use

Users must keep access to AGC System and to the connected QuickBooks company restricted to authorised AGC staff, and must not share sign-in details, keys or tokens.

4. Ownership

AGC owns AGC System. QuickBooks and Intuit are trademarks of Intuit Inc. AGC System is not made or endorsed by Intuit.

5. No warranty

AGC System is provided as is, for AGC's internal use. AGC staff remain responsible for reviewing invoices and accounting records.

6. Ending the connection

AGC can disconnect AGC System from QuickBooks Online at any time. See the disconnect page.

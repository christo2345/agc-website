AGC System for QuickBooks Online

Privacy policy

AGC System is an internal tool used only by American Graphic Contractors, LLC ("AGC") with AGC's own QuickBooks Online company. This page explains what it reads and how that information is handled.

What it reads

  • Invoices: number, date, due date, amount, balance and line descriptions.
  • Customers: name and billing email.
  • Products and services, payment terms and payment status.

It does not read payroll, employee, bank account or card information.

How it is used

Only to show invoice and payment status on AGC's projects and to create invoices that an AGC project manager has approved.

Where it is kept

In AGC's own project database and AGC's own Google Workspace account. The keys that keep the QuickBooks connection alive are stored in AGC's Google account and are not shared with anyone.

What we never do

  • We do not sell QuickBooks data or share it with other companies.
  • We do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Keeping and deleting

Information stays in AGC's project records for as long as AGC keeps those records. When AGC disconnects the app, the connection keys are deleted and no further data is read.

Contact

Questions or requests about this information: accounting@americangraphiccontractors.com.
